Hey Doll ❣️

I don't know if this little page can change anything. Maybe it can't.

But I wanted to make something myself... because somehow a normal message didn't feel enough.

♡ ───────── ♡

I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for every time I made you feel unwanted. I'm sorry for the moments when I failed to be the man you wanted me to be.

There are things I wish I had understood earlier. Things I wish I had done differently. And I know saying that now doesn't magically fix them.

🥹

One of the things I loved most was seeing you happy. Your cheerful side. Your dramatic side 😂. Even those little moments when you were annoyed at me and somehow I still wanted to make you smile.

I loved getting that tiny chance to do something that could instantly light up your mood.

🫶

And I loved how you cared about me. About my future. About our future.

I probably didn't always express how much that meant to me. But I noticed it. I remember it.

🙄😂

And yes... I know I teased you A LOT.

The sarcasm. The stupid jokes. Annoying you just to see that smile afterwards.

Half the time I was probably being irritating on purpose. The other half... okay fine, probably also on purpose. 😌

✦

If you've decided to move on...

I won't force you to stay.

I don't want your last memory of me to be someone desperately trying to hold you back.

If this is really where our paths separate, then I genuinely wish you an immense amount of success, happiness and peace.

I hope you achieve everything you ever wanted.

🌷

And please take care of yourself.

Protect yourself. Protect your heart. Protect your peace.

I always wanted to protect you in whatever way I knew how. Maybe I didn't always get it right.

But I hope you always remember that you deserve to be loved, respected and cared for.

🌙

And if someday you wonder...

I'm not that far away.

Not to disturb your life.
Not to hold you back.
Not to ask anything from you.

Just somewhere out there, quietly wishing the best for you.

♡

Doll ❣️

Whatever happens from here, I'm grateful that I got to know you, annoy you, make you laugh, and be a part of your life.
